Overview

Psychometrically Grounded AVI Assessment

Asynchronous video interviews (AVIs) let applicants record one-way answers to fixed questions. OPVA was collected as a mock management-traineeship application so that personality and interview performance can be studied under a standardized selection context, rather than from short, task-free social media clips. Interview questions follow Trait Activation Theory at the facet level: each of the eight past-behaviour prompts targets one Extraversion or Conscientiousness facet.

01

Facet-Activated Interview Protocol

Participants answered eight workplace past-behaviour questions (one per Extraversion facet: Social self-esteem, Social boldness, Sociability, Liveliness; one per Conscientiousness facet: Organization, Diligence, Perfectionism, Prudence). There are no generic interview questions in this release.

02

Expert-Validated Dual Labels

OPVA provides HEXACO-60 self-reports, observer-rated Extraversion and Conscientiousness at both factor and facet level (four trained raters, HEXACO-BARS), and recruiter-rated job competencies (two professional recruiters per participant).

03

Open Dataset for Research

The release includes interview videos, psychometric labels, demographics, and an official subject-level split. Academic researchers can request access after signing the user agreement. The same data underpin the CHB 2024 psychometric study and the IEEE TAC 2024 LLM evaluation.

Dataset

The OPVA Benchmark

Collection and annotation follow Zhang et al. (IEEE TAC 2024) and Koutsoumpis et al. (CHB 2024). This video release contains the 684 participants who have complete interview recordings. Zhang et al. (IEEE TAC 2024) report 685 labeled participants; one extra ID has labels but no video files.

Collection Protocol

  • Recruitment: Prolific (n = 889) plus a snowball sample (n = 58). Participants were excluded for corrupted media or annotations, failed attention checks, noncompliant HEXACO responding, or observer flags. The papers retain 685 labeled participants; this video release keeps the 684 with complete mp4 files.
  • Task: Simulated management traineeship AVI. Target length 1–2 minutes per answer; no pause or re-recording. Each participant was paid $7.
  • Questions: 26 candidate items were reviewed by three personality experts (facet ICC(2, 3) = 0.99). The final AVI uses one question per Extraversion or Conscientiousness facet (absolute agreement ICC(2, 3) = 1).
  • Self-reports: HEXACO-60 (10 items per domain) plus demographics (age, gender, education, work experience).
  • Annotation: Four independent raters scored Extraversion and Conscientiousness facets with HEXACO-BARS after the four trait-relevant questions. Six professional recruiters (two per participant) scored four competencies and overall hireability.

Protocol

Interview Questions

Question order is fixed. Each item is a past-behaviour prompt, contextualized at work, that activates one HEXACO facet of Extraversion or Conscientiousness. Factor scores in this release are the mean of the four corresponding facet scores.

# Interview question Target facet Factor
1 Remember a time when you were in a social setting where you didn't know any of the other people, and this affected the extent to which you felt (un)comfortable with yourself. Could you describe your personality in that situation? Social self-esteem Extraversion
2 Remember a time when you finished using equipment at work and you could put everything back in place (or not). Could you describe your personality in that situation? Organization Conscientiousness
3 Remember a time when you took part in a group discussion and you could assume a listening or leading role. Could you describe your personality in that situation? Social boldness Extraversion
4 Remember a time when a task was more challenging than usual and that you could decide to push yourself harder or leave it aside (or pass it to someone else). Could you describe your personality in that situation? Diligence Conscientiousness
5 Remember a time when you were invited to an after-work social event and you could decide to join or skip it. Could you describe your personality in that situation? Sociability Extraversion
6 Remember a time when a task required paying attention to details in order to avoid making mistakes. Could you describe your personality in that situation? Perfectionism Conscientiousness
7 Remember a time when your team members were feeling down and you could decide to raise their spirit (or not). Could you describe your personality in that situation? Liveliness Extraversion
8 Remember a time when you wanted something badly at work and you could act on an impulse (or not) in order to get it. Could you describe your personality in that situation? Prudence Conscientiousness
Labels

From HEXACO Facets to Job Competencies

Each participant has HEXACO-60 self-reports on all six domains (1–5). Observer personality ratings cover Extraversion and Conscientiousness at factor and facet level (the interview questions activate these two traits). Recruiters score four competencies plus overall hireability. All observer and recruiter scores use a 5-point BARS.

Papers

Publications using OPVA

There is not yet a standalone dataset paper. Please cite the two studies that introduced and evaluated this AVI corpus. Prompting and analysis scripts from the LLM study are in GPT_interview.

Can Large Language Models Assess Personality From Asynchronous Video Interviews? A Comprehensive Evaluation of Validity, Reliability, Fairness, and Rating Patterns

Tianyi Zhang, Antonis Koutsoumpis, Janneke K. Oostrom, Djurre Holtrop, Sina Ghassemi, Reinout E. de Vries. IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing, 15(3), 1769–1785, 2024.

Zero-shot evaluation of GPT-3.5 and GPT-4 on the 685 OPVA interviews, compared with a task-specific model and human annotators. LLMs reach similar or better validity for Extraversion, but show uneven trait performance, limited reliability, and some biases—especially for Conscientiousness and hireability.

IEEE Xplore / DOI Author PDF Code (GPT_interview)

Beyond traditional interviews: Psychometric analysis of asynchronous video interviews for personality and interview performance evaluation using machine learning

Antonis Koutsoumpis, Sina Ghassemi, Janneke K. Oostrom, Djurre Holtrop, Ward van Breda, Tianyi Zhang, Reinout E. de Vries. Computers in Human Behavior, 154, 108128, 2024.

Psychometric analysis of the same facet-activated AVI protocol. Models using words, facial expressions, and voice explain more variance in observer Extraversion / Conscientiousness and interview performance than in self-reports, with stronger prediction on trait-relevant questions, consistent with Trait Activation Theory.

Access

How to Obtain OPVA

OPVA is released for academic, non-commercial research. Please sign the user agreement and email it to Tianyi Zhang. Access is granted after the signed form is reviewed.

Request procedure

  1. 1Download the OPVA User Agreement and print or export it as PDF.
  2. 2The form must be signed by a researcher with a permanent position (e.g., professor or assistant/associate professor). Students may be listed as participants.
  3. 3Email the signed PDF to Tianyi Zhang. Include your name, institution, position, and a brief description of the intended research use.
